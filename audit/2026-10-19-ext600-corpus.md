```sh
uv run python scripts/export_ext600_corpus.py --cache "$SEMDEBT_DATA_DIR/rules-backfill/presentation-20261018-91-final/baseline_bank.jsonl" --output "$SEMDEBT_DATA_DIR/scorer-corpus/ext600.jsonl"

```
